How to Resize Photo for SSC & Railway Applications?

Uploading photos outside the required 20 KB to 50 KB range causes automatic rejection in Staff Selection Commission (SSC) and Railway (RRB) online forms. Use our dedicated SSC Photo Resizer or Railway Resizer to automatically optimize resolution to 3.5cm x 4.5cm with crisp clarity.

UPSC Signature & Photo Compression Rules

UPSC requires signatures to be between 20 KB and 300 KB with approximately 350x350 pixel aspect ratio. Our UPSC Resizer ensures zero distortion while maintaining optimal DPI.

NEET UG 2026 Postcard Size Photo Guidelines
